DigiFidget

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

DigiFidget is a collection of calm fidget toys. It is built to work entirely on your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. DigiFidget does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

What stays on your phone

Your settings and creations are saved only on your device so they are there next time you open the app. That includes sound and theme settings, favourites, the name and stickers on your toy box, and your best spinner speed. They never leave your phone, and deleting the app deletes them.

Photos

If you choose to share a picture of a rock stack, DigiFidget hands the image to your phone's own share sheet. The app does not access your photo library and does not keep a copy.

Motion and haptics

The dice can follow your phone's tilt and some toys use gentle vibration. This happens on your device only and nothing is recorded.

Children

DigiFidget collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
