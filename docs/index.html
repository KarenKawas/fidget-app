Common questions

There's no sound

Check that your phone isn't on silent, then open Settings (the gear) and make sure Sound effects is on and the volume is up.

The dice don't follow my tilt

Turn on Tilt on the dice table and allow motion access when your phone asks. You can always drag a die to throw it, or tap to roll.

How do I get to the toys without the box?

Tap the grid button at the top of the home screen to see every toy as a list.
